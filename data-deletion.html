Promotion Publisher Data Deletion

Promotion Publisher does not collect information about social-media visitors, followers, contacts or other users.

To revoke a connection, remove Promotion Publisher from the relevant platform's connected-app or business-integration settings. For Facebook, use Business Integrations settings. For Pinterest, revoke the application from the Pinterest account's connected-app settings.

To request deletion of any locally retained administrator credentials or operational records, send a message through the Jack Burgoyne Books Facebook Page or the Jack Burgoyne Books Pinterest profile stating “Promotion Publisher data deletion”. Requests will be completed within 30 days.
