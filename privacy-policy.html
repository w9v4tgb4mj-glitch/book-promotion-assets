Promotion Publisher Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: 9 August 2026

Promotion Publisher is a private publishing tool used by the administrator of the Jack Burgoyne Books Facebook Page to prepare and publish book-related posts.

Information processed

The tool processes only the Facebook Page identifiers, access credentials, permissions and publishing responses needed to post to the administrator-authorized Page. It does not request or collect information about Page visitors, followers or other Facebook users.

How information is used

Information is used solely to authenticate the Page administrator, publish requested Page content, record whether publication succeeded and maintain operational security.

Storage and sharing

Credentials and operational records are stored privately on the administrator's systems. Personal information is not sold, rented or used for advertising, and is not shared with third parties except Meta as necessary to operate the Facebook Pages API or where required by law.

Retention and deletion

Credentials are retained only while the integration remains active and may be revoked through Facebook at any time. Operational records are retained only as needed to prevent duplicate posts and diagnose failures. See the data deletion instructions.

Contact

Questions or deletion requests may be sent through the Jack Burgoyne Books Facebook Page.
