Promotion Publisher Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: 20 August 2026

Promotion Publisher is a private, single-user tool used by the administrator of Jack Burgoyne Books to prepare, schedule and publish book-related posts to administrator-authorized social-media accounts, including Facebook and Pinterest.

Information processed

The tool processes only the account, Page, board and Pin identifiers, access credentials, permissions and publishing responses needed to post to administrator-authorized destinations. It may retain the identifier and publication time of content it created to prevent duplicate posts and diagnose failures. It does not request or collect information about visitors, followers, private messages, contacts, advertising accounts, payment information or other social-media users.

How information is used

Information is used solely to authenticate the administrator, publish requested book-promotional content to the administrator's selected Page or board, record whether publication succeeded, prevent duplicate posts and maintain operational security.

Storage and sharing

Credentials and operational records are stored privately on the administrator's systems and are excluded from source control and public reports. Personal information is not sold, rented or used for advertising, and is not shared with third parties except the selected publishing platform, including Meta or Pinterest, as necessary to perform an administrator-requested publishing operation or where required by law.

Retention and deletion

Credentials are retained only while an integration remains active and may be revoked through the relevant platform's connected-app or business-integration settings at any time. Operational records are retained only as needed to prevent duplicate posts and diagnose failures. See the data deletion instructions.

Contact

Questions or deletion requests may be sent through the Jack Burgoyne Books Facebook Page or the Jack Burgoyne Books Pinterest profile.
